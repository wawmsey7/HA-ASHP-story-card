Heat pump history

The new card running on your heat pump's real readings from Thu 5 to Sun 8 Nov. The recording ends at Sun 18:20, so the card treats that moment as now.

Try this

  1. Find yesterday at 14:00 and see what the heat pump was doing.
  2. Zoom in on a single defrost, one of the blue ticks in the heat pump lane.
  3. Get back to now.
  4. At each step, say which stretch of time is on screen before checking the title.

More to try

  • Under Graphs & layers, switch the lane to Power strip, add the Power or Hot water tank graph, or show Hot water at full scale.
  • Tap a block in the heat pump lane for its times, energy and COP.
  • Tap a diamond under the lane to see what setting changed.
  • Tap the title for Today, Yesterday, the last 7 days or a date.
  • Drag the shaded part of the day row to move, or its ends to zoom.
